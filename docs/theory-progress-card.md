# Theory Progress Card

## Purpose

The sidebar now keeps a compact theory progress card visible during normal play.
It summarizes the four case-board issues, shows how many issue evidence slots are filled,
and gives the player a direct jump into the case-file theory tab.

## QA

- `desktop-theory-progress-card.png` verifies the desktop sidebar card.
- `desktop-theory-progress-jump.png` verifies the jump into the case-file theory board.
- `mobile-theory-progress-card.png` verifies the mobile layout.
